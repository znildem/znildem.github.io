AI-POWERED SKIN CANCER DETECTION

Supporting Early Awareness Through Machine Learning

Skin cancer is one of the most common and potentially fatal cancers worldwide, making early detection especially important. This project aims to develop an accessible mobile application that uses machine learning to analyze skin lesion images and provide preliminary real-time predictions of whether a lesion may be benign or malignant. The app is also intended to help users monitor changes in their skin health over time while encouraging appropriate medical consultation when needed.
